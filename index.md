## About me

I'm Deepthi (Dee) Rao Coppisetty. I lead Engineering and Security Fundamentals at GitHub, and my work has spanned software engineering, product, and technical program management.

My path into tech started with computer science, undergrad and grad school, at the University of Maryland. Since then I've worked across insurance, healthcare, and developer platforms. Working in tech has let me be part of many industries, helping technology scale and make a real impact on people's lives. That has meant scaling access to digital health in southeastern Wisconsin during the COVID pandemic, and now leading Engineering and Security Fundamentals at GitHub, home to more than 225 million developers. What drives me is learning, and solving today's problems for a better tomorrow.
